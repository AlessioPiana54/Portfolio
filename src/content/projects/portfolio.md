A portfolio built around one idea: two sides of the same person, the night and the day. The mark
in the corner is a yin-yang of the two — a crescent moon among the stars on one side, a small sun
on the other. The whole site follows it: switch the theme and the mark turns, the moon gives way to
the sun, the sky changes color and the hills catch the light.

Everything you see is drawn for this site: the mark, the illustrations of each project, the hills
and the clouds of the home page. They share a small palette and a flat, soft style so the pages
feel like one picture book.

It started as plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and was later migrated to **Astro**. Projects are now
Markdown files in a typed content collection, images are optimized at build time, and the site is
fully static, deployed to GitHub Pages by a workflow on every push.

Small details carry the rest: an animated wave on the footer border, a moon that follows its real
phase, gentle parallax and transitions that step aside when you prefer reduced motion.
